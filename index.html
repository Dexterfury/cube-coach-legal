Cube Coach: Learn & Solve — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Cube Coach: Learn & Solve ("the app", "we", "our") is a single-player puzzle app, developed by Raphael Daka, for playing twisty puzzles and learning to solve them. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and who it is shared with. The app has no user accounts and no login: your progress is stored only on your device, apart from the third-party services described below.

Data stored only on your device

The app keeps the following locally on your device and never sends it to us:

  • Your best times and fewest moves for each puzzle, and the puzzle you played last
  • Lesson progress, drill results and streaks
  • XP, level, daily streak and badges
  • Settings (sound, vibration, colour scheme) and whether you have seen the tour and welcome
  • Whether you own Pro, and how many free tries or ad rewards you have used

This data is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its storage from your device settings. We have no way to access, back up or recover it.

Camera

The optional Scan with camera feature uses your camera to photograph the faces of a real cube so the app can read the sticker colours. The camera permission is requested only when you open that feature. Photos are processed on your device, are not uploaded or shared, and are kept only as temporary files in the app's private cache. The app works fully without granting camera access: you can paint the colours by hand instead.

Advertising

The app can offer optional rewarded ads (an ad you choose to watch in return for one more use of a feature) using Google AdMob. Ads are never shown during play or when the app starts, and are never shown to players who own Pro. To show an ad, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects device and advertising identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data, and may use them to personalize ads, subject to your consent choices.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and similar regions), the app shows Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform) before requesting any ad, so you can choose whether ads are personalized. Ads requested are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from AdMob.

Purchases

The app offers one optional in-app purchase, Pro, processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details: Google handles all payment information directly.

Your purchase status is synced through RevenueCat, which assigns your device an anonymous identifier (not linked to your name, email or Google account) so that Pro is remembered across reinstalls and restored on your other devices. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

What we don't collect

  • No account creation, login, name, email or phone number
  • No contacts, microphone or precise location
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No data sold to third parties

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us using the details below and we will address it.

Data retention & deletion

Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. Advertising and purchase data are retained and deleted according to Google AdMob's and RevenueCat's own policies (linked above); we do not hold a separate copy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
